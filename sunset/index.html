What are Curated Pathways?

Curated Pathways step learners through learning from a collection of readings, courses, activities and media chosen by BC Public Service subject-matter experts. These curators provide you with guidance along the way.

Where do I find Curated Pathways?

You can now access your Curated Pathways for Equity, Diversity and Inclusion, Indigenous Learning and Respectful Workplace through the LearningHUB, and register for them on the PSA Learning System. Your pathway completion can now be connected to your learning record.

Visit the LearningHUB now for a full list of curated pathways.

I was following a pathway on this site. What do I do now?

If you were following a curated pathway on this web page, you will need to register for your curated pathway and continue the pathway in its new home. Visit the LearningHUB now, find your pathway, and register for it.

When you are finished your pathway, you will be able to be marked complete on your learning record in the PSA Learning System.

If you would like to have a curated pathway that you completed here appear in your learning record, you can find that pathway in the LearningHUB catalogue and register for it, then go to the pathway and mark all your steps complete.
